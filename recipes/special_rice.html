The recipe of classic rice pillaf

Description

Rice Pilaf is not just a dish; it is a specific method of cooking rice designed to create the perfect texture. Unlike plain boiled rice, which can be sticky or clumpy, the goal of a pilaf is to have every single grain perfectly cooked, fluffy, and separate from the others

The ingredients
  • 1 cup Long-Grain White Rice
  • 2 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • small Yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 1 clove Garlic, minced
  • 1¾ cups Chicken broth
  • ½ tsp Salt
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
preparation
  • Rinse the Rice:

    Place the rice in a mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water until the water runs clear. This removes excess starch, which prevents the rice from becoming sticky. Shake off excess water.

  • Sauté the Aromatics:

    In a medium saucepan or deep skillet with a tight-fitting lid, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for about 3-5 minutes until it is soft and translucent (do not brown it). If using garlic, add it in the last minute of cooking.

  • Toast the Rice

    Add the rice to the pan with the onions. Stir constantly for about 2-3 minutes. You want the rice grains to be coated in the butter and smell slightly nutty. The edges of the grains should start to look translucent

  • Simmer:

    Pour in the broth and add the salt. Increase the heat to bring the liquid to a boil.

  • Cover and Cook:

    Once boiling, immediately reduce the heat to low. Cover the pot tightly with the lid. Simmer gently for 15-18 minutes. Do not lift the lid during this time; the steam needs to stay trapped inside

  • Rest and Fluff:

    Remove the pot from the heat but keep the lid on. Let it sit and steam for another 5-10 minutes. This rest period allows the moisture to redistribute, ensuring the rice is fluffy, not mushy. Finally, remove the lid and fluff the rice gently with a fork. Garnish with parsley and serve.
